Rabhan analytics screen — implementation plan

4 phases to build a client-facing analytics experience matching the dark green luxury aesthetic shown in the mockup. Covers data architecture, UI components, real-time features, and PDF report vault.

Phase 1 · Foundation KPI snapshot + 6 engine health scores Weeks 1–2
Live KPI cards Top of analytics screen
Financials row
Total sales (SAR), net profit, and ad spend in a scrollable horizontal chip row. Each card shows delta vs. last period.
Efficiency row
ROAS, conversion rate, and CTR. Color-coded: green if above target, amber if within 10% below, red if critically off.
Volume row
Orders count, impressions, clicks, and add-to-carts. Collapsed by default, expandable inline.
Period picker
Last 7d / 30d / 90d / custom range. All cards and charts re-fetch on change. Persist selection per client in local state.
Design note
Match the home screen's dark green (#0A2818) card surface with gold (#C9A84C) accent for positive deltas — keeps the luxury aesthetic consistent across screens.
6 Growth Engines panel Below KPI cards
Engine health ring
Circular progress ring (0–100%) per engine. Tap opens a bottom sheet with the exact tasks Rabhan is doing to improve that engine.
3×2 engine grid
Store · Product · Ads · Sales Page · Operations · Analytics. Each tile shows engine name, score, and a one-line status ("On track" / "Needs attention").
Task drill-down
Bottom sheet lists active tasks for that engine: task name, assignee, status pill, and last-updated timestamp.
Score history sparkline
Mini sparkline in each tile showing score trend over last 30 days. No axes — just the shape of progress.

Phase 2 · Charts Sales trend + campaign tracking Weeks 3–4
Sales trend chart Matches home screen mini-chart, but full-width
Area chart
Full-width area chart with dark green fill and gold stroke — replicates the aesthetic from the home screen. Touch to inspect a specific date's value.
Overlay comparison
Toggle a ghost line for the previous period (dashed, 40% opacity). Lets the client visually verify growth without needing to read numbers.
Metric switcher
Chip row to switch the chart between Sales, Profit, ROAS, and Orders. Chart animates on switch (Reanimated layout transition).
Granularity toggle
Daily / Weekly / Monthly grouping. Collapses noisy daily data into smoother weekly view for cleaner storytelling.
Active campaigns list Scrollable below chart
Campaign card
Platform badge (Meta / TikTok / Google), campaign name, status pill (Active / Paused), budget bar (spent vs. allocated), and key stats: clicks, conversions, ROAS.
Platform filter
Filter chips at top of list: All · Meta · TikTok · Google. Filters apply instantly on the rendered list, no refetch needed.
Sort options
Sort by ROAS, spend, or conversions. Useful when client has 10+ active campaigns.
Deep link to ad manager
Each card has a subtle "View in Meta / TikTok" link that opens the native ad manager — gives power users the escape hatch they want.
Architecture decision
Pull campaign data from Meta Marketing API + TikTok Ads API server-side every 15 minutes and cache in Firestore. Don't call ad APIs client-side — rate limits and token management are painful on mobile.

Phase 3 · Reports vault PDF hub + formal document delivery Weeks 5–6
Reports vault screen Third tab or section within analytics
Report categories
Monthly reports · Weekly campaigns · Competitor analysis · Strategy docs. Horizontal filter chips, reports list below.
PDF card
Each report shows: type badge, title, upload date, account manager name, file size. Single tap previews inline. Long press to download.
Push notification trigger
When AM uploads a report, Firebase FCM sends a push: "Ahmed uploaded your April monthly report. Tap to view." Notification deep-links directly to that PDF.
Read receipts
AM dashboard shows whether client has opened each report and when. Creates accountability and natural follow-up triggers for the AM.

Phase 4 · Intelligence layer AI-generated insight summaries Weeks 7–8
Weekly AI summary card Pinned at top of analytics screen
Plain-Arabic summary
3–4 sentence narrative generated by LLM from raw KPI data. "This week sales grew 18% driven by your summer campaign. ROAS improved to 4.6x. One campaign is underspending — Rabhan will investigate."
Anomaly alerts
Detect unusual dips or spikes in any metric vs. trailing 7-day average. Surface as inline alert within the summary card with a brief explanation.
Ask your data
A text input in the analytics screen: "Why did conversions drop on Thursday?" — routes to a Claude API call with the client's last 30 days of data as context.
Auto-refresh cadence
Summary regenerates every Monday at 6 AM local time via a Cloud Function scheduled trigger. Client sees the latest summary on their Monday morning check-in.
Strategic value
This is the differentiation layer. Most agency apps are static dashboards. A client who gets a plain-Arabic weekly narrative they can actually read — without needing to interpret charts — will stay far longer.
